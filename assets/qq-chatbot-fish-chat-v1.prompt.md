# QQ ChatBot 程序图标设计 v1

使用内置 image_gen 生成。主题：聊天气泡与小鱼融合，白色主体、蓝靛渐变底、青色背鳍；无文字，适合小尺寸识别。

最终图稿：qq-chatbot-fish-chat-v1.png。Windows 包装：qq-chatbot-fish-chat-v1.ico，含 16、24、32、48、64、128、256 像素尺寸。ICO 仅做格式与标准尺寸编码，没有重绘图稿。

可在程序「专属人设 → 程序图标」选择 PNG 或 ICO。此文件是新设计，未覆盖当前自定义图标、角色形象或昵称。

## 初始生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished Windows desktop application icon for the independent personal AI companion app QQ ChatBot. Design a single original, memorable app icon: a friendly plump little fish ingeniously fused with a speech bubble into ONE bold coherent symbol. The fish is pearly white with one large simple dark indigo dot eye and a small friendly curved smile; its two broad tail lobes and the speech-bubble notch should read clearly, with no tiny decorative details. Put the white symbol centered on an opaque softly rounded square tile in rich periwinkle blue / indigo with a restrained soft gradient and a tiny aqua accent on one fin. Premium contemporary app identity, warm approachable personality, refined soft dimensional shading, nearly flat graphic clarity. Large clean silhouette, strong contrast, generous but compact optical padding, legible at 16 and 32 pixels. Orthographic straight-on composition, square 1024x1024 canvas, one icon only, tile fills about 90 percent of the canvas. Actual transparent alpha outside the rounded tile; tile interior remains opaque. No words, no letters, no typography, no watermark, no mockup, no device frame, no background scene, no extra bubbles or sparkles, no robot head, no penguin, no resemblance to the official Tencent QQ logo. Deliver a production-ready icon asset, not a presentation sheet.

## 最终调整提示词

Edit the supplied app icon. Preserve exactly the cute white plump fish/speech-bubble mascot with indigo eye and smile and aqua dorsal fin. Make the output an entirely OPAQUE FULL-BLEED SQUARE icon: extend its rich blue/indigo gradient background smoothly to all four edges and every corner of the square canvas. REMOVE the rounded-square tile outline and outside margin completely. There must be NO transparency, NO black surround, NO separate tile, NO rounded canvas corners, NO edge highlights, NO speckles or isolated pixels. The background is a simple continuous blue gradient filling 100% of the square. Keep the fish centered with balanced 10% safe padding, smooth premium soft-dimensional shading, very crisp and clear at small sizes. No text, no watermark. Final square production app icon, not a mockup.

最终采用不透明满幅蓝底，避免透明边缘杂点。中间透明版本未用作交付素材。
